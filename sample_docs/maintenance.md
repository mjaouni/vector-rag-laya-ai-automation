# AC maintenance — demo knowledge

Routine preventative maintenance may include replacing or cleaning suitable air filters, visual inspection by a professional, checking thermostat settings, and checking airflow around vents.
Homeowners can check whether the thermostat is set to cooling and whether the filter needs replacement, following manufacturer guidance. Do not work on high-voltage components or refrigerant lines yourself.
During a visit, licensed HVAC professionals may inspect electrical connections, condensate drainage, coils and refrigerant systems. Services vary by equipment.
